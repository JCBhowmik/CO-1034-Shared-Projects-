About Treasure Hunt

Treasure Hunt is an interactive, location-based quiz game designed for mobile devices. Players visit different locations, discover information and answer questions to earn points & compete with other players.

Game Features

Location-Based Questions

Explore real locations and discover clues to solve questions.

Interactive Quiz

Answer different types of questions to earn points.

Leaderboard

Compare your score with other players.

Timed Challenges

Complete the treasure hunt before time runs out.

How to Play

  1. Start the Treasure Hunt game.
  2. Read the question carefully.
  3. Visit the required location.
  4. Find the correct answer.
  5. Submit your answer to earn points.
  6. Compete on the leaderboard.
